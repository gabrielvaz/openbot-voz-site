Privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026.

OpenBot Voz has no server of its own, does not sell data and does not track you across apps or websites.

Conversations with your agents

Messages, files and answers go straight from your iPhone to OpenBot on your Mac over an encrypted connection. The OpenBot connection service only helps the two devices find each other and does not receive the content of your conversations.

Live voice

When you use live voice, microphone audio is sent to the voice service you chose (Google Gemini, OpenRouter or OpenAI) with the key you provided. That service handles the audio under its own privacy policy. The app does not store the audio on the device.

Keys and settings

Voice service keys and your settings stay in the iPhone Keychain and leave the device only to authenticate with the voice service you chose.

Notifications

To tell you when an agent finishes, the app may register a notification identifier with the OpenBot connection service.

Apple Watch

The watch exchanges data only with your iPhone, over watchOS's own channel.
